willie (he/him):
a curious living blob with immense curiousity and a deep desire to explore anything, even if it defies reality. he's the type of fellow to be spontaneous even when he's facing a risky situation.


lue (they/them):
a creature (combination of a tardigrade and a turtle) who rarely speaks, but loves taking on crazy adventures with willie. lue is the type to roll around in mid-air whenever willie is idle.

★ how did they meet? (backstory) ★

one night, small willie wanted to find this mystical rock that was only available in a dense forest. rumors had it that this forest was extremely dangerous and seldom visited a lot due to its high spike in radiation whenever there was a full moon. of course willie didn't mind it as he was prepared to face anything. luckily, this forest was highkey in his backyard. thus, willie went into this forest to see if he was worthy of finding this rock.

a few hours passed by and willie was lost. apparently, this rock was super secluded in the depths of the forest and no one was meant to find it in the first place. "what a piece of baloney!", willie exclaimed. willie wished for a companion, even if it was a leaf, to accompany him.

suddenly, a shiny, dazzling, and enthralling piece of something shined from willie's peripheral vision. at first, willie thought it was just another rock covered stardust laying around who knows where, but as he analyzed it more, he soon realized it was something truly novel. with care, he picked it up.
"AHH!!"
"AHHHH!!!"
this enticing rock turned out to be a living creature! there was a name tag near his body. the nametag prints "lue"

and the rest was history. (actually- quick relaspe: they decided to hang out for a bit. finding out that they both possessed the magical ability to tranverse around the entire universe! due to this, they agreed on becoming travel buddies.)
